# History Explorer

**[→ Open the app](https://sdono043.github.io/history-explorer/)**

An interactive history curriculum built to teach and share, currently covering two sections: the post-WWII world order and the founding of the United States.

## What's inside

### World History (1945 – Present)
- **Interactive timeline** — era bands, leader tenure bars, and event dots from 1945 to present
- **33 events** — from Bretton Woods to the AI race, each with strategic/economic analysis
- **22 key figures** — leaders with honest assessments of their decisions and consequences
- **5 Long Threads** — cross-era themes: Dollar Hegemony, China's Rise, Oil as Power, the Afghan Thread, Globalization Arc
- **Era filter** — narrow the timeline to any of five eras (Post-War Order → Multipolar Fracture)

### Early United States History (1763 – 1865)
- **Combined timeline** — era bands, presidential term bars, war bands, and event dots from the Road to Revolution through the Civil War
- **35 events** — from the Royal Proclamation of 1763 through Lincoln's assassination, spanning colonial unrest, the Revolutionary War, and the early republic
- **16 presidents** — Washington through Lincoln, each with party, term dates, and a detail write-up
- **Interactive battle map** — step or auto-play through the Revolutionary War's 10 major battles in chronological order, plotted by location and date
